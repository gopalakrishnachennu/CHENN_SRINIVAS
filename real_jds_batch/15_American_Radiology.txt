Description
The American College of Radiology (ACR) is a progressive membership organization representing nearly 40,000 medical specialists in radiological care.  As a member of our team, you will join a radiological world leader in patient-centered care, advocacy, policy, clinical research, and quality and safety.

If you share our core values of:
*Integrity *Visionary *Excellence *Leadership *Transparency *Member-Driven
We want you on our team!

Principal duties include:

Architect and Optimize: Design, maintain, and fine-tune AWS RDS (MySQL/PostgreSQL) and Amazon Redshift environments to ensure peak performance.
Manage Hybrid Infrastructure: Support MS SQL Server environments, including T-SQL scripting and SQL Agent jobs, across both on-prem and EC2-hosted instances.
Streamline Data Pipelines: Execute seamless data migrations and complex transformations between MS SQL Server, RDS, and Redshift.
Proactive Monitoring: Utilize Datadog and native AWS tools to monitor system health, preemptively identifying and resolving performance bottlenecks.
Champion Data Integrity: Partner with cross-functional teams to uphold rigorous standards for data security, availability, and compliance.
Standardize & Innovate: Maintain comprehensive technical documentation and drive continuous improvement through strategic database recommendations.
Qualified candidates will possess the following:

The ideal candidate will be located in the US mid-Atlantic region.
3+ years of related experience preferred. 2+ years of hands-on experience with AWS RDS MySQL, and Amazon Redshift (development, tuning, monitoring, maintenance) required.  2+ years of experience with MS SQL Server technologies, including T-SQL, SQL Agent jobs, and EC2 required.
Proven experience in database/data migration between MS SQL Server and AWS RDS/Redshift.
Familiarity with Datadog for monitoring and alerting.
Strong initiative and ability to work independently with minimal supervision.
AWS Certified Database – Specialty, etc.
Experience with scripting languages for automation (Python and Powershell).
Knowledge of data warehousing concepts and ETL pipelines.
