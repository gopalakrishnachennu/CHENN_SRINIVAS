**Role Overview**
We are seeking a Senior Database Reliability Engineer to join our Core Infrastructure Team. This team is responsible for managing all of BILL's infrastructure and databases. You will play a key role in architecting, building, and supporting high-throughput data platforms and ensuring exceptional data consistency and performance across databases, streaming, and distributed systems.

---

**Key Responsibilities**

* Design, build, scale, and support mission-critical data platforms.
* Ensure data consistency and performance at the database level, primarily using Kafka and Flink.
* Architect and build resilient, fault-tolerant real-time data pipelines.
* Collaborate with engineering, product, and infrastructure teams to create scalable and high-performing solutions.
* Manage enterprise RDBMS (PostgreSQL, Aurora, MySQL, Oracle) databases, focusing on optimization, security, and access control.
* Implement high availability, replication, CDC, disaster recovery, and multi-region database deployments.
* Work with tools like GoldenGate, Debezium for replication, and use IaC with Terraform for managing infrastructure.
* Troubleshoot complex platform issues across the database, streaming, and infrastructure layers.
* Monitor and alert using DataDog, Splunk, and CloudWatch to resolve performance issues.

---

**Required Qualifications**

* 5+ years of experience with enterprise RDBMS (PostgreSQL, Aurora, MySQL, Oracle).
* 3+ years managing databases & CDC Streaming services on AWS.
* Experience with Apache/Confluent Kafka (running clusters at scale with exactly-once or at-least-once delivery).
* Familiarity with Flink pipelines (stateful streaming, checkpointing, windows).
* Experience in database replication tools (GoldenGate / Debezium).
* Proficient in Python and other programming languages.
* Strong experience with query optimization and performance tuning.
* Knowledge of monitoring tools like DataDog, Splunk, and CloudWatch.
* Expertise in containers and cloud platforms (Kubernetes/ECS/AWS).
* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field, or equivalent experience.
